Contract rates

Manual entry

Enter a separate contract rate for each night, UPG room type, and occupancy. Thursday and Friday nights can have different prices from other nights. Currency is fixed by the selected breakdown language: English = USD; Hebrew = ILS. Rates are saved locally for each group.

Night dateRoom type (UPG)OccupancyContract rateCurrency (automatic)

Enter the date of the night the rate applies to. For example, a stay from Thursday to Sunday has Thursday, Friday, and Saturday night rows. Thursday and Friday may use different rates. English contract rates and Folio comparisons are always USD. Hebrew contract rates and Folio comparisons are always ILS. No exchange rate or currency conversion is applied. If an older saved rate is marked in the other currency, re-enter its amount in the required currency or delete and recreate that row. The Group Report room type is taken from UPG, not RM. Master-account VAT (18%) is excluded from room-charge reconciliation.

Generate Excel breakdown

Uses the original hotel templates

Create a breakdown for the selected group. Room charges are grouped by stay night, room type and occupancy. Existing Free/Staff and incidental sections are retained without adding unconfirmed calculations.

Select a group and enter its nightly contract rates.

English room charges are in USD; the English incidental section remains in ILS. Hebrew breakdowns are in ILS and retain the template's existing 18% VAT formulas. No exchange-rate conversion is performed. A Tourist group using the Hebrew template is blocked because that template automatically calculates VAT, while Tourist VAT must remain manual.
